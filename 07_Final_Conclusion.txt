FINAL CONCLUSION

Both Merge Sort and Quick Sort correctly sort the given fixed-length keys:

102, 125, 147, 218, 275, 324, 389, 456

Merge Sort provides O(n log n) time in the best, average and worst cases,
but requires O(n) additional space.

Quick Sort has O(n log n) average-case time and can be efficient in practice,
but its worst-case time is O(n^2) when poor pivots are repeatedly selected.

For large fixed-length keys where predictable performance is important,
Merge Sort is an appropriate choice because its worst-case time remains
O(n log n).
